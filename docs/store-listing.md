## Full description

SplitEase helps friends, roommates, and trip groups share expenses without the spreadsheet chaos.

• Create groups and add friends.
• Split equally, by amount, percent, or shares.
• See who owes whom with simplified balances.
• Record settlements and pay via UPI, PayPal, or Venmo links.
• Import bank CSV transactions.
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online.
• Available in multiple languages.

Money is calculated with exact decimal math — no floating-point surprises.
